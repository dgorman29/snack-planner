# Features

Current behavior of the app. Update this file whenever a feature is added, changed or removed.

## Look and feel
- Cozy "Peach Jam" theme: blush background, outlined cards with a flat shadow, rounded Fredoka headings. Tabs are in a floating dark bar at the bottom; the current tab shows its name. The Log tab shows a count badge when prepped or cooked recipes need a rating.

## Install as an app
- Installable as a standalone app (Chrome on Android: menu > Install app). Opens full-screen with its own icon. Requires being online; there is no offline mode.

## Login
- Email and password sign-in (Supabase Auth). Accounts are created by the owner in the Supabase dashboard; public sign-up is disabled.
- All signed-in users share the same data. "Sign out" is next to the week label.

## Recipes: dinners and snacks
- Every recipe lives in the Library. A recipe tagged **Weekday dinner** is a dinner; every other recipe is a snack.
- Dinners are never offered as snack suggestions. Snacks are never offered when planning dinners.

## Week tab
A Dinners / Snacks toggle at the top. The header badge shows "N of 5 planned" on Dinners and "N snacks chosen" on Snacks. A "See the shopping list" button appears once anything is chosen.

### Dinners
- One slot per weekday, Monday to Friday. A dinner planned on a day is also chosen for the week, so it feeds the shopping list and the Log.
- **Tonight** card (shown on weekdays) highlights today's dinner with "Swap dinner" and "Mark cooked". If nothing is planned for today it offers "Pick a dinner".
- The other days are tiles. Tap a tile's title to pick or swap its dinner, or tap an empty tile ("Add a dinner"). The round check marks that dinner cooked; a logged rating shows on the tile.
- The picker lists the library's dinners. Dinners already on another day are listed last with that day's name. "Clear <day>" empties the slot.
- "Fill empty days from my library" fills every empty weekday using the same cooldown and variety rules as snack suggestions (below). It may fill fewer days if too few dinners qualify, and says so.
- "Clear week" (below the tiles, shown when at least one dinner is planned) empties all five days after a confirm step. Cleared dinners also leave the shopping list and the Log; ratings already logged stay in History and on the recipes. Snacks chosen for the week are not touched.
- With no dinners in the library, the tab points to the Library to add one and tag it Weekday dinner.
- Dinners are planned from the library only; there are no AI-generated dinner ideas yet.

### Snacks
- "Suggest snacks" builds six suggestions: half from the library, half generated by Claude. "More ideas" appends three; "New set" replaces the list.
- "Library only" toggle skips the AI and picks only from saved snacks.
- Filters: "Chosen (N)" shows only the snacks chosen for this week, including any a later "New set" dropped from the suggestions (it hides "More ideas" while on); the tier filters Grab and go, 2-3 min and Weekend batch combine with it.
- Cards expand to show steps and ingredients. Selecting a card adds it to the week; selected cards can be marked prepped. Selected AI ideas are saved to the library automatically.

### Suggestion rules (`pickFromLibrary`)
- Cooldown: a recipe is skipped for 3 weeks after it was made, or 1 week if its average rating is 2.6+ (loved).
- Previously refused recipes resurface after 3 weeks with a "Worth another try" note.
- Untried recipes get a score boost; longer gaps raise the odds.
- Vegetables are spread out so one vegetable isn't suggested twice in a set when avoidable.

## List tab
- Built from every chosen recipe, dinners and snacks together. Duplicate ingredients (same name and unit) are summed, and rows used by several recipes say "for N recipes".
- Grouped by produce, dairy, pantry, frozen, other.
- Sections for hand-typed ingredients (Add by hand, or new lines in Edit): saved first with a keyword guess, then Claude sorts any names it hasn't seen and the app remembers the answer, so each name is asked about once. If Claude can't be reached the keyword guess stays. Recipes from AI ideas, a link or a photo get their sections from Claude when they're created.
- Check items off, mark "have it" (remembered in the pantry list), add manual items, and "Copy as text".

## Log tab
- Prepped snacks and cooked dinners can be rated Loved it, Ate some, or Refused. Ratings drive future suggestions. Dinner cards show their day ("Tue dinner") and say "cooked" instead of "prepped".

## Library tab
- 14 seeded snacks (no seeded dinners). Search by title, vegetable or ingredient. Filter pills: All, Weekday dinner, Snacks. Delete recipes.
- Tap a recipe to expand it: shows the description (steps), ingredients and recipe link.
- "Edit" (shown when a recipe is expanded) changes its name, recipe link, snack timing, description, time to prepare in minutes, and ingredients (one per line, e.g. "2 tbsp olive oil"; unchanged lines keep their shopping-list section, new ones are sorted by Claude, see List tab). Changes show on the Week tab and shopping list too; past weeks in History keep the name the recipe had then.
- Add from a photo: "From a photo" on the Add a recipe sheet takes up to 4 photos (camera or gallery) of a cookbook page, recipe card or handwritten note. Photos are shrunk on the phone, then Claude transcribes the recipe with its quantities. The same "Weekday dinner" pill applies.
- Add by URL: Claude reads the page and extracts ingredients and quantities. A "Weekday dinner" pill on that sheet tags the import as a dinner and starts it without a snack timing.
- Add by hand: name, optional recipe link (a link typed on the first screen carries over; a bare "example.com/x" gets https:// added), main vegetable, optional "Weekday dinner" tag, snack timing, description (how to make it) and one ingredient per line.
- Snack timing (Grab and go, 2-3 min, Weekend batch) is required for snacks and optional for dinners: turning the Weekday dinner tag on clears it, and for a dinner tapping the selected timing again clears it (also in Edit). A dinner with no timing simply shows no timing label.
- Add, edit and picker popups open centered on the screen, scroll inside themselves when taller than the screen, and close with the X or the Escape key.
- "Weekday dinner" tag: toggle it on any recipe from the library list.
- "Add to this week" on a snack selects it for the current week (it appears on the Snacks view and feeds the shopping list). On a dinner the button reads "Add to a day" and asks which weekday. Tap "In this week" to take either back out (a dinner also leaves its day). A new set of snack suggestions replaces the Snacks cards but a pinned snack stays selected and on the list and log.

## History tab
- Exposure meter per vegetable, out to 10 tries, with a "warming up" flag when recent ratings improve.
- Past weeks with each verdict. Weeks roll over automatically on Monday, which also clears the planned dinners.

## Known limits
- No per-user data: every signed-in account sees and edits the same household data.
- All data is one shared set of rows, so simultaneous edits from two devices are last-write-wins.
- Dinners cover Monday to Friday only, one dinner per day.
- No automated tests.
